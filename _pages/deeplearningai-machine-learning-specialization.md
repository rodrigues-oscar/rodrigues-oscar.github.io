I completed the DeepLearning.AI Machine Learning Specialization on Coursera in November 2023. 

This program offered an extensive and hands-on learning experience in the field of data science. Throughout the courses, I delved into data analysis, data visualization, and machine learning, gaining practical skills that are highly valuable in today's data-driven world. Working with real-world datasets and industry-standard tools, I honed my ability to extract insights from data, create compelling visualizations, and build predictive models.

To view my certificate of completion awarded to me by Coursera and DeepLearning.AI, please click [here](/assets/certificates/ibmdatasciencecertificate.pdf).

Skills learned include: `Classification`, `Clustering`, `Data Analysis`, `Data Mining`, `Data Science`, `Data Visualization`, `GitHub`, `Jupyter Notebooks`, `Machine Learning`, `Matplotlib`, `NumPy`, `Pandas`, `Python`, `Recommender Systems`, `Regression`, `SQL`, `SciPy`, `Scikit-learn`, `Seaborn`.
